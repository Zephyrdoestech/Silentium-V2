# 🎵 SILENTIUM

> *"I will wield what I hold to drive back the darkness, and I will hunt the reason for every shadow, until my family is finally, truly avenged."*

**SILENTIUM** is a story-driven, turn-based RPG built in Java using the **LibGDX** framework. Set in a world plunged into an "Eternal Silence," players take on the role of young musicians who must use forgotten instruments as weapons to fight back against the Shadow Beasts and restore sound to the world.

---

## 📖 About the Game

Centuries ago, a once-musical town fell silent to let their guardian rest. However, this absolute silence gave birth to terrifying creatures known as Shadow Beasts. When a lone bell accidentally rings and shatters the quiet, the beasts awaken in a frenzy.

As a child of the silent era who has never known sound, you are drawn to uncover the truth. You must fight through the Ruined Town of Echoes, descend into the Silent Caverns, and confront the silence-devouring entity in the Abyss of Dissonance.

## ✨ Key Features

*   **Top-Down Exploration:** Smoothly navigate through decaying towns, dark caverns, and twisting abysses.
*   **Musical Combat System:** A unique turn-based battle system where notes (A-G) determine your damage output.
*   **The Metronome System:** Synchronize your note attacks with the ever-changing Metronome tempo to multiply your damage.
*   **Chord Combos:** Play specific 3-note chords (e.g., C Major, D Minor) to cast heals, apply damage buffs, and generate shields.
*   **Time-Pressure Battles:** Think fast! Each turn has a strict 15-to-20-second time limit.
*   **Dynamic Loot & Items:** Defeat enemies to earn game-changing drops like the *Crimson Chorus* or *Silent Barrier*.

## 🎻 Characters

Choose your protagonist, each with their own unique instrument, passive abilities, and story arc:

1.  **Sonara (The Banjo - *Scourge of Echoes*)**
    *   *Playstyle:* Aggressive, high damage, reflects damage back to enemies.
    *   *Story:* Driven by grief and a fierce need for vengeance after her protective delusion was shattered.
2.  **Aurelius (The Flute - *Whispering Bulwark*)**
    *   *Playstyle:* Defensive, healing, shields the weak.
    *   *Story:* A gentle soul burdened by his family's selfish legacy, fighting to become the moral compass of a new era.
3.  **Lyron (The Harp - *Shattered Harmony*)**
    *   *Playstyle:* Strategic, relies on perfect Metronome synchronization for massive buffs.
    *   *Story:* Once deemed the weakest of his family, he fights through crippling fear to become a custodian of memory.

## 🎮 Controls

### Exploration
*   **W / Up Arrow:** Move Up
*   **S / Down Arrow:** Move Down
*   **A / Left Arrow:** Move Left
*   **D / Right Arrow:** Move Right

### Combat
*   **Keyboard / Mouse:** Select Notes (A, B, C, D, E, F, G) to build your attack.
*   **Inventory:** Access items during your turn before the timer runs out.
